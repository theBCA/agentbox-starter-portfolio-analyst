# Commentary examples

## A quarter behind the benchmark

Draft:

> Markets were choppy this quarter and the portfolio struggled a little, but
> we remain confident that the positioning will pay off as rates come down.

To the rule:

> The portfolio returned 2.1% against 3.4% for the benchmark, 1.3 points
> behind. The two largest contributors to the gap were Nordic Semiconductor
> (weight 9%, return -12%) and the cash position (weight 8%, return 0.9%).
> One risk worth watching: technology is 31% of the portfolio against 22% in
> the benchmark.
> Figures as of 2026-09-22; past performance is not a guide to future
> returns.
