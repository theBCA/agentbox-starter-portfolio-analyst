# How to explain a move

A market comment has four parts, always in this order, and nothing else:

1. **The move against the benchmark**, as two numbers: the portfolio's return
   and the benchmark's return over the same period, then the difference.
2. **Two contributors**, the positions that explain most of the difference,
   each with its weight and its return over the period.
3. **One risk** worth watching, stated as a fact about the portfolio (a
   concentration, a currency, a sector) rather than a prediction.
4. **The closing line**, exactly: `Figures as of <date>; past performance is
   not a guide to future returns.`

No forecasts, no recommendations, no adjectives about the market's mood. A
comment that says what will happen next is not a comment we send.
