# Words we may use

Three rules apply to every sentence a client will read:

- **No buy, sell or hold language.** We describe; we do not advise. "Consider
  adding", "we like", "attractive entry point" and their cousins do not
  appear. A client who asks what to do is told that a person will call.
- **Past performance is labelled** every time a return is quoted, with the
  exact phrase `past performance is not a guide to future returns`.
- **Concentration is named.** Any single position above 20% of the portfolio
  is named as such, with its weight, in the first paragraph, whether or not
  the client asked.

Numbers carry their date. A return without a date is not a number we send.
