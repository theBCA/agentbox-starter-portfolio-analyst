# Wording examples

| Breaks the rule | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| "We recommend adding to Equinor at these levels." | "Equinor is 6% of the portfolio and returned 9% over the month." |
| "The fund returned 12% last year." | "The fund returned 12% in 2025; past performance is not a guide to future returns." |
| "The portfolio is well diversified." (with Novo Nordisk at 24%) | "Novo Nordisk is 24% of the portfolio, above our 20% concentration line." |
| "Tech should recover in the second half." | "Technology is 31% of the portfolio against 22% in the benchmark." |
| "Hold on, this is temporary." | "A person from the team will call to discuss the position." |
